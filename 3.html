From artificial neurons to handwriting

Three milestones in the emergence of neural networks.

  1. 1943

    McCulloch and Pitts introduced a mathematical neuron model, laying a theoretical foundation for neural networks.

  2. 1958

    Frank Rosenblatt demonstrated the perceptron, an early model that could learn from data.

  3. 1989

    Yann LeCun and colleagues published neural-network results for recognizing handwritten ZIP code digits.
